### 2026-07-13 — Competitive & market landscape
**Question:** How does Vellum stand out among Notion / Obsidian / Coda et al.?
**Findings:** No single competitor combines local-first + native graph + frictionless inline
math + live spreadsheet embedding — the whitespace is the intersection. Frictionless,
no-modal math is a real (narrow, high-intent) wedge vs mainstream apps and a smoothness
argument vs Obsidian+plugins. The Vellum↔Tessera live-model embed is near-novel (Coda is the
closest, but cloud-only). The knowledge graph is mature — don't headline it. Full write-up:
[2026-07-13-market-landscape.md](./2026-07-13-market-landscape.md).
**Recommendation:** GTM with "the STEM note app that doesn't fight you" (inline math);
differentiate with "docs with live models, not dead numbers" (Tessera bridge); wrap in "the
calm, local-first suite."
